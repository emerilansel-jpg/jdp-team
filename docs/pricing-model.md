# Pricing Model — Credit Wallet (owner-approved, 2025)

This document is the single source of truth for how Thine Solutions prices work. All public
copy (index.html, homepage2.html, services.html, calculator.html, proof.html, motion.js)
must match this spec.

## Core mechanic

- **1 Credit = $0.01 USD.**
- **Wallet credits never expire.** One-time top-ups roll over forever.
- One wallet pays for everything: crew hours, video editing, SEO content, and any
  à-la-carte service on the rate card.

## Credit packs (one-time top-ups)

| Pack        | Price   | Credits   | Bonus        |
|-------------|---------|-----------|--------------|
| Starter Pod | $499    | 50,000    | —            |
| Growth Pod  | $999    | 110,000   | +10k bonus   |
| Scale Partner | $1,899  | 220,000   | +20k bonus   |

## Service rates (paid from the same wallet)

- Junior staff hour: 300 cr ($3.00)
- Senior specialist hour: 500 cr ($5.00)
- SEO article (1,500 words): 3,200 cr ($32.00)
- Other à-la-carte services per the rate card.

## Optional monthly crew time (subscription)

- Client picks a role + a monthly credit amount (e.g. Junior VA, 10,000 credits/month ≈ 33 hours).
- The amount is **deducted from the wallet on the same date each month (prepaid)**.
- **Only unused subscription hours expire at the end of that monthly period** — the time
  was reserved from the shared team.
- Change or stop anytime, effective at the next monthly deduction (**4 days' notice**).
- **Low balance protection:** auto top-up from the card on file so the subscription
  deduction never fails. The client can turn this off anytime — the plan then simply
  pauses until the wallet is topped up.

## Refunds

- Credits are **non-refundable** and cannot be exchanged for cash.
- They also never expire, so there is no pressure to spend fast.

## History

- Supersedes the previous "monthly credit allocation refreshes every 30 days / no
