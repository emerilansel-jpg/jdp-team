# JDP.team Pricing — Direct-Service Storefront (no credit system)

> **MODEL CHANGE (2026-09-30):** The credit system was DROPPED. Clients buy
> services DIRECTLY with dollars, like an online store (Amazon × Fiverr hybrid).
> One product, two ways to buy: **one-time (a-la-carte)** or **subscription**.
> NO "credits", NO "wallet", NO "top-up", NO "never expire" — anywhere.

## Core rule

Same service, two purchase modes. Bigger monthly commitment = cheaper per hour.

- **Base rate:** Junior $3/hr, Senior $5/hr.
- **Subscription ladder** (per-hour rate):
  - 160 hr/mo = base rate
  - 120 hr/mo = base +15%
  - 80 hr/mo = +15% on top of the 120 level
  - 40 hr/mo = +15% on top of the 80 level
- **A-la-carte (one-time)** = 40-hr subscription hourly rate × 1.35
  (subscription is ALWAYS cheaper per hour than a-la-carte).

## Final rate table

| Level | Junior $/hr | Junior $/mo | Senior $/hr | Senior $/mo |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **A-la-carte (one-time)** | $6.16 | per-task | $10.27 | per-task |
| **40 hr/mo** | $4.56 | $182 | $7.61 | $304 |
| **80 hr/mo** | $3.97 | $318 | $6.61 | $529 |
| **120 hr/mo** | $3.45 | $414 | $5.75 | $690 |
| **160 hr/mo** | $3.00 | $480 | $5.00 | $800 |

(160-hr junior monthly = 160 × $3 = $480; 160-hr senior = 160 × $5 = $800.)

## Payments

- **One-time** products → PayPal one-time checkout.
- **Subscription** products → PayPal Subscription (recurring plan), auto-charge
  flat monthly until the client cancels (4-day notice; unused sub hours expire,
  non-refundable). Cancel/pause is manual by the client.
